A full classroom day (~7.5 hours of content + breaks). Times are indicative — see the
[Trainer pacing guidance](../trainer/) for how to flex each block to your cohort.

| Time | Block | Module B | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 08:45 | Welcome, intros, objectives | — | Set the CFS scene |
| 09:00 | **Challenge 00** — Setup, tooling & the CFS brief | _foundation_ | Codespace + OIDC + agents/MCP |
| 09:45 | **Challenge 01** — Requirements Assessment | B.1.1 | Agent-driven discovery |
| 10:30 | ☕ Break | — | 15 min |
| 10:45 | **Challenge 02** — Solution Design + Well-Architected Review | B.2.1 + B.2.2 | HLD, ALZ design areas, 2 WAF pillars |
| 12:00 | 🍽️ Lunch | — | 45 min |
| 12:45 | **Challenge 03** — Deploy the Secure Hub & Spoke | B.3.1 + B.3.2 | **Start gateways first**, then continue |
| 14:15 | ☕ Break | — | 15 min |
| 14:30 | **Challenge 04** — Private networking, isolation & private compute | B.3.2 | Private Link, App Gateway + WAF, Front Door |
| 15:45 | **Challenge 05** — Validation & Testing | B.4.1 | Connection Monitor, sign-off |
| 16:45 | ☕ Break | — | 10 min |
| 16:55 | **Challenge 06** — As-Built, Operations & Teardown | B.4.2 | Evidence pack + tear down |
| 17:45 | Retrospective & close | — | Re-delivery tips for trainers |

<Aside type="caution" title="Long-running deployments">
  Azure VPN / Virtual WAN gateways take 30–45 minutes to provision. Challenge 03 has you
  **kick those off first** and continue working while they build. The agenda assumes this
  parallelism — do not wait idle.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Half-day variant">
  For a half-day "design-only" delivery, run Challenges 00–02 and demo 03–06 from the
  [solution keys](../solutions/). The Trainer guide describes the cut points.
</Aside>
