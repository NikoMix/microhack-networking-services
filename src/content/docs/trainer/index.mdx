This section equips a trainer to **re-deliver** the microhack. The detailed facilitator
guide, timing sheet, talk track, inference-backend setup, and reset/troubleshooting
runbooks are added here by the enablement workstream.

Core trainer assets:

- **Facilitator guide** — block-by-block delivery notes and gotchas
- **Timing & pacing** — full-day and half-day variants
- **Inference backends** — switching agents between GitHub Models / Azure OpenAI / Azure AI Foundry
- **Reset & troubleshooting** — recover a stuck deployment, re-run a challenge, clean up
- **Slides** — the Marp deck in [`/slides`](https://github.com/NikoMix/microhack-networking-services/tree/main/slides)

Until your class, dry-run [Challenge 03](../challenges/03-deploy-hub-spoke/) once to warm
subscription registrations, and review the [Prerequisites](../prerequisites/).
